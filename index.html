CHAPTER I — ABOUT

A physician-scientist with a global vision.

Born in East Germany, Dr. Rick Wolthusen combines European scientific rigor, American clinical excellence, and a deep commitment to communities across Africa. He is a Staff Psychiatrist with the Schizophrenia and Bipolar Disorder Inpatient Program at McLean Hospital and an Instructor in Psychiatry at Harvard Medical School.

After earning his MD in Germany, Rick completed a Master of Public Policy at the Harvard Kennedy School and his psychiatry residency at Duke University — uniquely equipping him to work at the intersection of medicine, policy, and health systems.

As Co-Founder and Director of the non-profit On The Move e.V., he champions brain health, fights stigma, and builds community mental health capacity in Ghana and Kenya — in partnership with the University of Ghana and international collaborators such as Deconstructing Stigma.
